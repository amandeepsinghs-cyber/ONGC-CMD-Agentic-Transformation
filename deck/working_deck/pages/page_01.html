What you will see today

Three live agents. Lower cost, higher revenue, stronger governance.

Agentic Transformation can transform ONGC into an Agentic organisation — boosting revenue, decreasing costs, while enabling robust financial and people governance.

01 · Decrease cost

Sagar DrishtiReal-time drilling agent

Watches the well as it is drilled, warns the engineer before the mud-weight window closes, and acts once a human approves. Less non-productive rig time, fewer kicks, and the paperwork done automatically.

02 · Boost revenue

UrviSick-well & workover agent

Spots wells that are declining or have stopped, explains why, and ranks the next intervention by barrels recovered. Earlier action on brownfield wells means more oil and gas sold.

03 · Financial & people governance

YuktiFinance & HR intelligence agent

One governed agent across finance and workforce data. Leaders ask in plain language and get answers with numbers, sources and role-based access built in.
