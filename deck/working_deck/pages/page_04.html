Well log digitisationTurning dark data into usable data

Decades of well logs and well completion reports exist only as scanned images. Engineers can see them, but no analysis and no AI can use them. An agent in Gemini Enterprise digitises them on request.

It looks simple, but it matters to every geoscientist who works with legacy logs.
