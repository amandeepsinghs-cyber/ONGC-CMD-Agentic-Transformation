Numbers behind the story

Drilling budget ₹15,150 Cr (FY27 plan): ₹9,055 Cr development + ₹6,095 Cr exploratory; ₹16,565 Cr actual in FY26. PSUWatch, 31 Aug 2026, CMD press briefing.
NPT 22.7% → 18.7% (FY23 → FY24): “NPT in drilling rigs reduced to 18.7% as compared to 22.7% in FY’23”. ONGC Corporate Presentation, 28 Jun 2024, p.14 (published on ongcindia.com; archived copy, 21 Nov 2024).
Drishti AI value (illustrative; NPT is a share of rig time, used as a proxy for spend): repeating FY24’s 4-point NPT gain ≈ ₹600 Cr a year (₹15,150 Cr × 4% = ₹606 Cr); cutting NPT losses 20% ≈ ₹570 Cr a year (₹15,150 Cr × 18.7% × 20% = ₹567 Cr).
Proof point: ONGC’s Real Time Drilling Operations Centre: 158 interventions, 393 rig days saved, ₹65.5 Cr notional benefit. ONGC Annual Report FY26, p. 99.
Deepwater: USD 125–150 Mn per deepwater well (PIB, 3 Aug 2026); 87 deepwater wells planned by FY31 (New Indian Express, 31 Aug 2026).
Urvi AI scenarios: 1% / 3% / 5% of FY26 crude + gas sales (₹1,20,140 Cr) = ₹1,200 / ₹3,600 / ₹6,000 Cr a year. Production 20.5 MMT crude, 20.0 BCM gas. ONGC Annual Report FY26, p. 21.
Workovers: 2,320 wells serviced in FY26; ₹2,191 Cr workover operations expense. Fields decline 6–7% a year (PIB). ONGC Annual Report FY26.
₹40,000 Cr people cost and capex: employee benefits ₹10,211 Cr (ONGC Annual Report FY26) + capex plan ₹30,000 Cr (PSUWatch). 1% ≈ ₹100 Cr and ₹300 Cr. Workforce ~24,000 (BRSR, p. 139).
Production chart (Urvi AI): ONGC standalone crude oil production FY21–FY26: 20.27, 19.55, 19.58, 19.47, 19.60, 19.33 MMT (Yukti AI data, from ONGC Annual Reports FY25 p.12 and FY26 p.53). “Urvi AI” bar = FY26 + 1–5%, illustrative.
Digitised log (slide 4): produced by running the Well Log Digitisation Agent’s pipeline on the Schlumberger sample scan (7,000.5–7,299.5 ft, 599 samples, 7 curves) and rendered with the agent’s own chart specification. Values are traced estimates, not tool measurements. LAS file: deck/assets/data/well_log_schlum.las.
