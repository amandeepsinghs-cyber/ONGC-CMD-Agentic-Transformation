Numbers behind the story

Drilling capex: FY26 ₹16,565 Cr (₹9,496 Cr development + ₹7,069 Cr exploratory, of ₹35,878 Cr capex); FY27 plan ₹15,150 Cr (₹9,055 Cr + ₹6,095 Cr, of ₹30,000 Cr capex, revised down ~16%). PSUWatch, 31 Aug 2026. Audited FY26 cash spend on exploratory and development drilling: ₹11,976 Cr, ONGC Annual Report FY26, p.298 (cash flow).
NPT 22.7% → 18.7% (FY23 → FY24): “NPT in drilling rigs reduced to 18.7% as compared to 22.7% in FY’23”. ONGC Corporate Presentation, 28 Jun 2024, p.14 (published on ongcindia.com; archived copy, 21 Nov 2024).
NPT at risk (illustrative; NPT is a share of rig time, used as a proxy for spend): NPT taken at ~20%, a round ballpark between ONGC’s 22.7% (FY23) and 18.7% (FY24); their average is 20.7%. FY26 drilling capex ₹16,565 Cr × 20% ≈ ₹3,300 Cr a year. Deepwater programme to FY31: ₹1 lakh Cr × 20% ≈ ₹20,000 Cr.
Proof point: ONGC’s Real Time Drilling Operations Centre: 158 interventions, 393 rig days saved, ₹65.5 Cr notional benefit. ONGC Annual Report FY26, p. 99.
Deepwater: ₹1 lakh Cr through FY2030-31, ~₹20,000 Cr a year, 87 wells (8 in FY27 rising to 27 in FY31), CMD at press briefing, PSUWatch, 31 Aug 2026. Separate from the government’s ₹84,084 Cr Samudra Manthan scheme, which ONGC has not yet decided to draw on. USD 125–150 Mn per deepwater well, PIB, 3 Aug 2026.
Urvi AI scenarios: 1% / 3% / 5% of FY26 crude + gas sales (₹1,20,140 Cr) = ₹1,200 / ₹3,600 / ₹6,000 Cr a year. Crude oil ₹83,810 Cr + natural gas ₹36,330 Cr; quantity sold 18.79 MMT crude, 15.49 BCM gas (standalone). ONGC Annual Report FY26, p.21. 1–5% are illustrative scenarios.
Workovers: 2,320 wells serviced in FY26; ₹2,191 Cr workover operations expense. Fields decline 6–7% a year (PIB). ONGC Annual Report FY26.
₹40,000 Cr people and capital spend (spend governed, not value claimed): FY26 expenditure on employees ₹1,02,107 Mn = ₹10,211 Cr; 23,117 employees, ONGC Annual Report FY26, p.22 (also Note 35.1, p.352). FY27 capex plan ₹30,000 Cr, PSUWatch, 31 Aug 2026. Sum ₹40,211 Cr. Every 1% better decision ≈ ₹100 Cr + ₹300 Cr ≈ ₹400 Cr a year (illustrative).
Production chart (Urvi AI): ONGC standalone crude oil production FY21–FY26: 20.27, 19.55, 19.58, 19.47, 19.60, 19.33 MMT (Yukti AI data, from ONGC Annual Reports FY25 p.12 and FY26 p.53). “Urvi AI” bar = FY26 + 1–5%, illustrative.
Digitised log (slide 4): produced by running the Well Log Digitisation Agent’s pipeline on the Schlumberger sample scan (7,000.5–7,299.5 ft, 599 samples, 7 curves) and rendered with the agent’s own chart specification. Values are traced estimates, not tool measurements. LAS file: deck/assets/data/well_log_schlum.las.
